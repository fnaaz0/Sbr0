# Privacy Policy for SBR Super-App

Last updated: August 2026

## 1. Information Collection
SBR Super-App does not collect, log, or share any personal identity information. All data and notes created inside the app are stored locally on your device.

## 2. AI Processing Data
When you interact with the integrated AI modules (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini), your text queries are encrypted and securely transmitted directly to the respective official AI serverless endpoints solely to generate responses. Your inputs are never sold or stored by us.

## 3. Data Protection
We utilize secure client-to-server routing to safeguard secure network payloads. Users retain 100% ownership of their text inputs and calligraphy generations.
